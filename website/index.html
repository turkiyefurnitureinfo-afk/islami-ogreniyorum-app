What the app offers

🕌

Prayer Times

Accurate namaz (prayer) times for cities across Türkiye and Europe, with a live countdown to the next prayer.

💬

Community Q&A

Ask questions about faith and life, and get helpful answers from fellow Muslims and AI assistance.

📰

News & Events

Stay up to date with community events, iftar gatherings, and project developments.

⚙️

Personalized

Switch between Turkish and English, choose a theme, and customize prayer-time notifications and sounds.
